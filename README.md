# EDS 222 HW 2

## Get started

Begin by cloning this repo to your directory on Workbench. Do not change the name! In order to receive credit and feedback, your folder name must match this repo's name exactly. Make sure to complete all the exercises on Workbench - that's what we'll use for grading and feedback. 

## Part 1: Linear regression

Demonstrate your understanding of linear regression by completing the exercises in `linear-regression.qmd`. You'll use soil lead data from the neighborhoods around the former Exide battery recycling facility to explore Simpson's paradox, fit and interpret models with interactions, and simulate data from a model.

The rubric by which your work will be assessed is in `linear-regression-rubric.md`.

Please note: some details are deliberately unspecified. For example, in section 3 you will choose your own parameter values for a simulation. Use your judgement to make appropriate choices for these details.

## Part 2: Final project proposal

Keep building toward your final project! `proposal.md` contains prompts that move you from exploring your data to planning your analysis: refining your question, drawing a DAG of the causal relationships, and stating your hypotheses. You'll respond in a new Quarto document named `proposal.qmd`, building on your exploratory analysis from `eda.qmd`. Like all final project checkpoints, the proposal is ungraded. That said, investing time in it now and getting feedback early will pay off greatly later this quarter!
